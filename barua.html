Barua — a letter home

You’ve been to Tanzania. Before it settles into memory, tell us what you carried home.

Not a review. A letter. Write to us the way you’d write to a friend who asked how it was — and if you allow it, we may one day share your words with someone standing where you once stood, wondering whether to go.

“I thought I was going to see animals. I came back having been somewhere quieter than I knew existed. The morning the migration crossed the river, no one in our vehicle said a word for an hour. I still haven’t, really.”

A traveller · after the northern Serengeti

If it helps, a few things you might tell us
  • The moment you keep returning to.
  • Someone — a guide, a face, a stranger — who stayed with you.
  • What you’d say if a friend asked whether they should go.
A few sentences is plenty. Write as much or as little as feels true.

We kept what you had begun — pick up wherever you left off.

However you’d like to appear — a first name, a city, or nothing at all.

The places, the month — only if you wish.

If you name a single place, it may appear as a quiet pin on our map of letters — never your identity, only the place.

That didn’t send — but your words are safe here. Please try once more.

Your letter goes to the Enkiama team first. Nothing appears anywhere without your blessing. Asante — thank you.

Your letter is with us.

We read every one — not as feedback, but as the last chapter of a journey we were lucky to be part of. Thank you for trusting us with it.

Kwaheri, until the next plain. — The Enkiama team
