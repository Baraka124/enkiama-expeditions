# Ngorongoro Conservation System

Ngorongoro is implemented as a conservation-landscape system rather than a crater-only destination page.

## Phase 1 — Governed conservation data
- `data/ngorongoro.json` governs five contexts, route shapes, planning factors and operated evidence.
- The public page distinguishes Ngorongoro Crater from the much wider Ngorongoro Conservation Area.

## Phase 2 — Context intelligence
- Crater floor
- Crater rim & highland forest
- Olmoti, Empakaai & volcanic highlands
- Olduvai, Laetoli & deep time
- Pastoral landscape

Each context exposes strongest use, planning implications, trade-offs and what type of traveller it best serves.

## Phase 3 — Route logic
Four journey roles:
- crater-focused
- crater + highlands
- Ngorongoro → Serengeti
- Ngorongoro → Rift / Natron

## Phase 4 — Ngorongoro composer
Inputs:
- available Ngorongoro time
- traveller priority
- onward journey direction

Outputs:
- whether to keep Ngorongoro focused
- whether a highland/archaeology/lived-landscape layer earns its place
- onward routing logic

## Publication discipline
- Do not guarantee black rhino, Big Five completion or specific wildlife sightings.
- Do not claim crowd-free crater experiences.
- Do not hard-code crater access times without current NCAA verification.
- Treat walking and archaeological access as subject to current rules and guiding requirements.
- Do not reduce pastoral communities to a generic cultural performance.
- Always distinguish the crater from the wider conservation area.
