# Ruaha Remote Safari System

Ruaha is Enkiama's remote / southern-Tanzania / repeat-safari prototype.

## Phase 1 — Governed remote-safari data
- `data/ruaha.json` governs ecological systems, seasonal windows, stay depth, access, traveller fit and route composition.
- No operated Enkiama Ruaha journey is published because no verified case currently exists in the public journey dataset.

## Phase 2 — Landscape intelligence
- Great Ruaha River system
- Mwagusi & sand-river country
- Miombo & special-antelope country
- Usangu & wider south-west

Each exposes strongest window, wildlife pattern, crowd profile, experience and trade-off.

## Phase 3 — Traveller fit + composition
- first Tanzania safari
- repeat safari
- walking-focused traveller
- short-trip suitability
- photography / predator focus

Route shapes:
- Ruaha as the safari
- Ruaha + Nyerere
- Ruaha + western Tanzania

## Phase 4 — Ruaha composer
Inputs:
- Ruaha nights
- safari experience
- travel period
- priority

Outputs:
- whether Ruaha should anchor the journey
- whether available time is too short
- stay-depth guidance
- season and activity caveats

## Publication discipline
- Do not promise crowd-free wildlife experiences.
- Do not guarantee wild dogs, lions, elephants or special antelope sightings.
- Do not hard-code flight routes, frequencies or transfer times.
- Treat walking/night drives as camp-, guide- and season-dependent.
- Do not describe green-season Ruaha as equivalent to late dry season.
- Do not create an operated Enkiama case study until a verified trip record exists.
