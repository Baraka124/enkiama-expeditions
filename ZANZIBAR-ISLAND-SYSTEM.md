# Zanzibar Island System

Zanzibar is Enkiama's coast / island-system prototype.

## Phase 1 — Governed island data
- `data/zanzibar.json` governs island zones, seasonal windows, stay depth, activities, traveller fit, route shapes and operated evidence.
- The page treats Zanzibar as several distinct coast/urban contexts rather than one generic beach destination.

## Phase 2 — Zone intelligence
- Stone Town & west
- North · Nungwi / Kendwa
- North-east · Matemwe / Mnemba-facing coast
- East & south-east · Paje / Jambiani / Bwejuu
- South & south-west

Each exposes swimming, tides, atmosphere, strongest use and trade-offs.

## Phase 3 — Stay / activity / traveller fit
- stay depth from 2 nights to 5–7 nights
- swimming, diving/snorkeling, kitesurfing, Stone Town and locally guided island context
- safari recovery, honeymoon, family, marine and culture-first traveller fit

## Phase 4 — Zanzibar composer
Inputs:
- swimming priority
- tide tolerance
- desired atmosphere
- available nights

Outputs:
- first coast direction
- closest alternative
- whether Stone Town deserves a proper chapter
- whether a single coast is stronger than a split stay

## Operated evidence
- Couple journey: Stone Town followed by Nungwi.

## Publication discipline
- Do not describe all Zanzibar beaches as equivalent.
- Do not guarantee all-day swimming outside exact coast/property context.
- Treat tides as a practical planning factor, especially on the east and south-east coast.
- Do not guarantee marine visibility, dolphin encounters or specific reef wildlife.
- Do not hard-code marine-transfer times or seasonal operator availability.
- Keep Stone Town heritage distinct from generic 'spice island' marketing.
