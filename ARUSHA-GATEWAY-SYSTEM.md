# Arusha Gateway System

Arusha is Enkiama's arrival / gateway / acclimatisation prototype.

## Core roles
- Arrival & reset
- Arusha National Park
- Mount Meru
- Coffee & highland context

## Stay logic
- 0 nights: direct transit
- 1 night: strong default
- 2 nights: useful depth
- 3+ nights: selective

## Composer
Inputs:
- available nights
- main purpose

Outputs:
- transit, buffer or deeper Arusha role
- Mount Meru and Arusha National Park caveats

## Publication discipline
- Do not present Arusha as only a transfer town.
- Do not imply Arusha National Park substitutes for a major safari circuit.
- Do not describe Mount Meru as an easy acclimatisation walk.
- Avoid exact transfer times without current traffic/road context.
