# Tanzania Master Discovery System

Tanzania is implemented as a country-level routing layer rather than a generic destination overview.

## Seven journey systems
- Mountain
- Northern wildlife
- Rift corridor
- Southern safari
- Western expedition
- Coast & island
- People & society

## Decision discipline
Route selection starts from:
- month
- available days
- first vs repeat traveller
- desired activity mix
- tolerance for transfer complexity
- coast / no-coast intent

## Publication discipline
- Do not reduce Tanzania to the northern circuit.
- Do not publish one universal 'best time'.
- Do not imply remoteness automatically means exclusivity.
- Route by traveller fit before destination fame.
