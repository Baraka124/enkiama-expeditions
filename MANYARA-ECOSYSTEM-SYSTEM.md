# Lake Manyara Compact Ecosystem System

Lake Manyara is implemented as Enkiama's compact-ecosystem / Rift-transition prototype.

## Phase 1 — Governed ecosystem data
- `data/manyara.json` governs habitat systems, seasonal windows, stay depth, activities, route roles and operated evidence.
- The page separates Lake Manyara National Park from the broader Rift Valley / biosphere context.

## Phase 2 — Habitat intelligence
- Groundwater forest
- Acacia woodland & floodplain
- Lake edge & wetlands
- Escarpment & southern hot-spring country

Each habitat exposes strongest use, wildlife pattern, crowd profile, experience and trade-off.

## Phase 3 — Route role
Three route roles:
- first safari day
- Tarangire → Ngorongoro bridge
- wider Rift-transition chapter

The park is included only when it has a specific job in the sequence.

## Phase 4 — Manyara composer
Inputs:
- available time
- traveller priority
- onward destination

Outputs:
- half-day / full-day / overnight guidance
- whether Manyara is optional
- whether the wider Manyara / Mto wa Mbu / Rift context earns additional time

## Publication discipline
- Do not guarantee tree-climbing lions.
- Do not guarantee flamingo concentrations or fixed shoreline conditions.
- Distinguish national-park boundaries from the broader UNESCO biosphere reserve.
- Treat canopy walk and night-drive availability as current-condition dependent.
- Do not describe a half-day visit as representing the whole park.
- Avoid exact transfer times without current route context.
