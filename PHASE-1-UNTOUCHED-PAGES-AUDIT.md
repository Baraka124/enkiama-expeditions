# 3. Phase 1 findings

## A. Country / route architecture

### `tanzania.html`
**Current strength**
- Strong strategic content.
- Already thinks in “journey systems,” not a destination catalogue.
- Headings such as “Seven Tanzanias can sit inside one map” and “Turn the country into a route” are directionally aligned with the benchmark.

**Current weakness**
- High card count and dense system language.
- Risks feeling like a planning interface rather than an immersive country page.
- Media references exist, but many appear to be part of shared header/menu systems rather than visible narrative fields.

**Phase 2 priority:** **P1**

---

### `parks.html`
**Current strength**
- Excellent conceptual premise: “Choose by experience, not fame.”
- Strong comparison logic across safari families.

**Current weakness**
- Very large file and high card density.
- Can easily feel like a taxonomy or decision matrix rather than premium editorial travel.
- Needs a stronger image-led architecture and a clearer visual distinction between safari “grammars.”

**Phase 2 priority:** **P1**

---

### `great-rift.html`
**Current strength**
- Strong story angle: corridor, ecology, history, movement.
- Better suited to Enkiama’s route-intelligence differentiation than a conventional destination page.

**Current weakness**
- Current structure still reads like the same system used across other destination pages.
- Needs a more linear, geographic experience: road, escarpment, communities, Natron, transition.

**Phase 2 priority:** **P1**

---

### `society-and-culture.html`
**Current strength**
- One of the most important differentiators for Enkiama.
- Strong content architecture around Kiswahili, Kilimanjaro, pastoral life, Zanzibar and lived context.

**Current weakness**
- Visually this needs to feel completely different from a park page.
- It should rely on people, place, domestic detail, food, streets, farms and encounters rather than safari UI patterns.
- Current content density is high enough that a standard destination template would weaken it.

**Phase 2 priority:** **P1**

---

# 4. Northern circuit / gateway

### `arusha.html`
**Current strength**
- Useful premise: Arusha as pressure absorber and gateway, not a mandatory attraction.
- Good operational intelligence.

**Current weakness**
- Extremely high card count.
- Visually likely to feel like a planning dashboard.
- Needs a calmer editorial framing around arrival, reset, Meru, coffee, local context and route departure.

**Phase 2 priority:** **P2**

---

### `tarangire.html`
**Current strength**
- Strong seasonal argument: “The river decides.”
- Clear route role.

**Current weakness**
- Similar page architecture to Serengeti / Ngorongoro / Manyara.
- Needs a distinct visual identity: baobabs, elephants, dry river system, seasonal concentration, quieter camp rhythm.
- Current design risks interchangeable “premium destination page” treatment.

**Phase 2 priority:** **P1**

---

### `manyara.html`
**Current strength**
- Excellent ecological contrast angle.
- Compact-park logic is useful.

**Current weakness**
- Current system is still content-heavy and card-led.
- Needs forest → escarpment → water → Rift transition as visual sequencing, not explanation only.

**Phase 2 priority:** **P2**

---

### `ngorongoro.html`
**Current strength**
- Strongly avoids reducing Ngorongoro to only the crater.
- Excellent conservation / highland context.

**Current weakness**
- Must visually distinguish:
  - crater floor;
  - rim;
  - highlands;
  - pastoral context;
  - route movement.
- Current page can still read like an information system instead of a place with altitude, scale and social context.

**Phase 2 priority:** **P1**

---

### `serengeti.html`
**Current strength**
- Very strong content.
- Seasonal motion and regional differentiation are already sophisticated.
- “Five Serengetis inside one ecosystem” is strategically strong.

**Current weakness**
- The page should be one of the most immersive on the site.
- It needs stronger photographic sequencing and temporal movement.
- Current system risks over-explaining what should partly be felt.

**Phase 2 priority:** **P1**

---

# 5. Southern / western Tanzania

### `ruaha.html`
**Current strength**
- Strong positioning around distance, staying longer and remoteness.

**Current weakness**
- Card density remains high.
- Needs a visual identity that immediately feels different from northern parks:
  - scale;
  - dry landscape;
  - long-distance access;
  - camp rhythm;
  - fewer transitions.

**Phase 2 priority:** **P2**

---

### `nyerere.html`
**Current strength**
- Strong river-led concept.
- Boating / walking / road gives it natural visual variation.

**Current weakness**
- Needs those modes to become the actual page rhythm.
- Current architecture remains too similar to other park pages.

**Phase 2 priority:** **P2**

---

### `mahale.html`
**Current strength**
- One of the strongest conceptual opportunities on the site.
- Remote access, lake, forest, chimpanzees and health/access constraints naturally create a distinctive expedition story.

**Current weakness**
- Current card/system structure can flatten what should feel remote, quiet and rare.
- Needs a very different page grammar from safari pages.

**Phase 2 priority:** **P1**

---

# 7. Coast / urban

### `zanzibar.html`
**Current strength**
- Strong distinction between coasts, history and route role.
- Avoids treating Zanzibar as a generic beach extension.

**Current weakness**
- Needs visible contrast between:
  - Stone Town;
  - east / north coast;
  - Pemba / quieter alternatives;
  - water;
  - food / people;
  - post-safari pace.
- Current structure is still too similar to inland destination pages.

**Phase 2 priority:** **P1**

---

### `stonetown.html`
**Current strength**
- Very strong content premise: “A city read on foot.”
- Potentially one of the most editorially distinctive pages.

**Current weakness**
- Needs urban rhythm, architecture, doors, courtyards, alleys, people, food and history to dominate.
- Must not look like a safari destination page with a city image.

**Phase 2 priority:** **P1**

# 13. Phase 1 conclusion

The untouched pages are **not empty or weak in content**. In many cases the content strategy is already strong.

The main weakness is that too many of them currently share the same **information-system grammar**:
- large heading;
- explanatory sections;
- cards / matrices;
- route logic;
- CTA.

Phase 2 should therefore not rewrite them from zero.

It should make each page family visually and spatially distinct while preserving the strongest existing content.

The goal is not:
> make every page more luxurious.

The goal is:
> make each part of Tanzania feel structurally different while still belonging to one Enkiama system.
