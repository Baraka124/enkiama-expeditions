# Stone Town Urban Heritage System

Stone Town is Enkiama's historic-city / cultural-urban prototype.

## Phase 1 — Governed urban heritage data
- `data/stonetown.json` governs city layers, stay depth, visit modes, route composition and operated evidence.
- The page treats Stone Town as a living Swahili trading city rather than a monument checklist.

## Phase 2 — Urban intelligence
- Lanes, courtyards & urban fabric
- Houses, doors & building traditions
- Trade, markets & everyday commerce
- Slavery, abolition & memory
- Seafront, food & evening

Each layer exposes strongest use, what to notice, experience, best fit and trade-off.

## Phase 3 — Time and method
- 3–4 hours: orientation only
- 1 night: strong default
- 2 nights: preferred for depth
- 3+ nights: selective

Visit modes:
- guide-led orientation
- independent wandering
- food / evening
- monument interiors where available

## Phase 4 — Stone Town composer
Inputs:
- available city time
- interest
- guide preference
- onward route

Outputs:
- whether Stone Town should be a focused visit, 1 night, 2 nights or a deeper city stay
- how guided interpretation and independent time should be balanced
- onward coast logic

## Operated evidence
- Couple journey: Stone Town before Nungwi.

## Publication discipline
- Do not describe Stone Town as a museum or frozen historic district.
- Do not reduce architecture to simplistic door-symbol folklore.
- Do not sensationalise slavery history or present memorial sites as attractions.
- Do not hard-code monument access status.
- Do not imply every traveller needs two nights.
- Keep Stone Town distinct from generic 'spice island' or beach marketing.
