# Tarangire Wildlife System

Tarangire is the second implementation of Enkiama's reusable wildlife-destination model after Serengeti.

## Phase 1 — Governed wildlife data
- `data/tarangire.json` governs landscape zones, seasonal windows, stay depth, activities and operated evidence.
- The page treats Tarangire as a water-driven system rather than a baobab/elephant postcard.

## Phase 2 — Landscape intelligence
- Tarangire River corridor
- Silale Swamp & eastern wetlands
- Baobab woodland & ridges
- Southern Tarangire
Each exposes strongest window, wildlife pattern, crowd profile, experience and trade-off.

## Phase 3 — Operated evidence
- July 2026 five-traveller journey
- operated couple journey
- Hakuna Matata three-traveller journey

## Phase 4 — Tarangire composer
Inputs:
- travel period
- available Tarangire time
- priority: elephants, birding, landscape or quieter safari

Outputs:
- route-depth recommendation
- season-specific caveat
- whether a day trip, one night, two nights or deeper stay makes sense

## Publication discipline
- Do not present peak dry-season wildlife concentration as year-round.
- Do not guarantee elephant herd size or predator sightings.
- Guided walking and night drives remain conditional on authorised operating context.
- A day trip should not be described as representing the whole park.
- Avoid exact transfer times without current road and camp context.
