# Mahale Expedition System

Mahale is Enkiama's remote expedition / chimpanzee / Lake Tanganyika prototype.

## Phase 1 — Governed expedition data
- `data/mahale.json` governs expedition systems, seasonal windows, stay depth, access, health protocol, traveller fit and route composition.
- No operated Enkiama Mahale journey is published because no verified public case currently exists.

## Phase 2 — Expedition intelligence
- Chimpanzee trekking
- Montane forest
- Lake Tanganyika
- Mahale Mountains

Each exposes strongest window, ecology, planning constraints, experience and trade-off.

## Phase 3 — Protection + traveller fit
- respiratory-health rules
- ranger-directed viewing distance
- controlled viewing/group limits
- behaviour / food restrictions
- first vs repeat traveller fit
- active-traveller fit
- short-trip rejection logic

## Phase 4 — Mahale composer
Inputs:
- Mahale nights
- fitness / comfort with steep humid trekking
- expedition priority
- Tanzania experience

Outputs:
- whether Mahale belongs in the itinerary
- whether available time is too short
- whether trekking demands make it a poor fit
- stay-depth and route guidance

## Publication discipline
- Do not guarantee chimpanzee sightings or trekking duration.
- Do not state that Mahale has no road access; limited dry-season 4x4 access exists, though air + boat is the normal traveller pattern.
- Do not publish exact chimp viewing-time or group-size limits without current TANAPA verification.
- Treat lake and hiking activities as weather-, guide- and camp-dependent.
- Do not promise solitude or exclusive chimp encounters.
- Do not create an operated Enkiama case study until a verified trip record exists.
