# Nyerere River Safari System

Nyerere is Enkiama's river / activity-diverse southern-safari prototype.

## Phase 1 — Governed river-safari data
- `data/nyerere.json` governs ecosystem systems, seasonal windows, stay depth, activities, traveller fit and route composition.
- No operated Enkiama Nyerere journey is published because no verified case currently exists in the public journey dataset.

## Phase 2 — Ecosystem / activity intelligence
- Rufiji River
- Lakes, channels & floodplain
- Woodland & savanna
- Walking country

Each exposes strongest window, wildlife pattern, crowd profile, activity value and trade-off.

## Phase 3 — Traveller fit + composition
- first safari
- repeat safari
- family suitability
- short safari
- safari + coast

Route shapes:
- Nyerere as the safari
- Ruaha + Nyerere
- Nyerere + Zanzibar

## Phase 4 — Nyerere composer
Inputs:
- Nyerere nights
- activity priority
- safari experience
- onward route

Outputs:
- whether Nyerere should anchor the safari
- whether two nights are too compressed
- activity-depth guidance
- onward southern/coast logic

## Publication discipline
- Do not guarantee wild dogs, lions, elephants or river wildlife.
- Do not imply boat safaris operate identically across all camps and seasons.
- Treat walking as camp-, guide-, age- and season-dependent.
- Do not promise solitude.
- Do not hard-code flight schedules or transfer times.
- Do not create an operated Enkiama case study until a verified trip record exists.
