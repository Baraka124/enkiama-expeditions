# Great Rift Corridor System

This page is the prototype for Enkiama's complex-region pages.

## Phase 1 — Corridor hub + governed data
- `data/great-rift.json` separates places, journey directions, decision factors and operated evidence.
- The page treats the Rift as a corridor rather than a single destination.

## Phase 2 — Place intelligence
- Engaruka: archaeology and historic irrigation landscape.
- Lake Natron: wetland ecology and Lesser Flamingo breeding significance.
- Ol Doinyo Lengai: active volcano; ascent remains conditional.
- Rift-floor communities: human landscape, not an attraction layer.

## Phase 3 — Operated evidence
- July 2026 is surfaced as a real Enkiama journey where Natron became one of the strongest memories.
- Historical and ecological claims are separated from Enkiama judgement.

## Phase 4 — Rift composer + onward logic
- Tests whether Natron earns its place based on time, traveller interest and tolerance for rougher road days.
- Can recommend excluding Natron rather than forcing it into an itinerary.
- Connects onward to Kilimanjaro, Society & Culture, Serengeti and Ngorongoro.

## Publication discipline
- Do not promise flamingo concentrations or breeding visibility.
- Do not present Ol Doinyo Lengai as an automatically available climb.
- Do not romanticise the corridor as empty or undiscovered.
- Check current road, weather and volcanic conditions close to travel.
