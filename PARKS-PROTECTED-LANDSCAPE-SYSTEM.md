# Parks Protected-Landscape Chooser

Parks is Enkiama's protected-landscape decision layer.

## Purpose
The page does not rank Tanzanian parks by a universal "best". It shortlists landscapes according to traveller priorities, broad route region and available time.

## Governed data
- `data/parks.json`
- Serengeti
- Tarangire
- Ngorongoro
- Lake Manyara
- Ruaha
- Nyerere
- Mahale
- Arusha National Park

## Decision inputs
- primary interest: migration, predators, elephants, birding, walking, boating, chimpanzees or first-safari breadth
- route family: north, south or west
- time available for the protected landscape

## Safari families
- Northern circuit: density, breadth and overland composition
- Southern circuit: longer stays, walking and boating
- Western expedition: forest, primates and access complexity

## Publication discipline
- Do not rank parks by a universal "best".
- Treat minimum-night values as planning guidance, not rules.
- Do not guarantee species sightings.
- Use activity and season fit before destination fame.
